# Prompts to try

Connect your agent (see the README), then try one of these. Good results come from asking the agent to look at its work with `render_view` and fix what it sees.

- *Build a cozy reading corner: an armchair, a floor lamp with a lathe shade, a side table with a stack of books and a mug. Render it from two angles and fix anything floating or intersecting.*
- *Make a medieval well: a round stone base (lathe), a wooden roof on two posts, a crank, and a bucket hanging on a rope (tube).*
- *Create a terrain with seed 12 and place five pine trees and a small cabin on land. Use probe_height so nothing floats.*
- *Model a classic Ionic column with a fluted shaft (sweep of a star-like cross section) and a capital with two scrolls (tube along a spiral path).*
- *Turn the current scene into a night scene: dark blue background, warm light color, and add glowing lanterns.*
- *Build a roller coaster: a closed track along a looping path (two tubes as rails plus box sleepers) and a train of three cars.*
- *Import this OBJ, put it on a pedestal and set up a nice camera.*
